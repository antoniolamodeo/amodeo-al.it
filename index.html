About

I work on the detection and classification of cataclysmic variables — binaries in which a white dwarf accretes matter from a companion — combining Gaia astrometry and photometry, the J-PLUS multi-filter survey and the published literature, under the supervision of Prof. Alessandro Ederoclite (Javalambre Observatory). Papers are signed AMODEO, A. L. By day I am a data scientist; by night, when the sky allows, an astrophotographer.
